    Where it came from

    A hand-drawn sketch: a 55 × 110 mm block, two mounting holes 91.5 mm apart, a wiring pocket and an angle marked "?". Every dimension in the model is tagged as written on the sketch, measured off it, or a design choice.

    What you are seeing

    The real printable files, loaded straight from the repository and animated in your browser. Not yet test-printed: the install steps show the planned procedure. Designed for Ring-style doorbell cameras; not affiliated with Ring.

    Get the files

    STLs, the OpenSCAD source, measurements and build scripts: github.com/ardyrobot/3d-print-ring-camera
