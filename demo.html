Where it came from

A hand-drawn sketch: a 55 × 110 mm block, two mounting holes 91.5 mm apart, a wiring pocket and an angle marked "?". Every option shares those fixed measurements from one file, mount_common.scad.

What you are seeing

The real printable files, loaded straight from the repository and animated in your browser. Not yet test-printed: the install steps show the planned procedure. Designed for Ring-style doorbell cameras; not affiliated with Ring.
